About Me

I am a Staff Research Engineer at Google DeepMind in New York City, where I develop AI systems that learn from multimodal, real-world data. My research brings together audio, vision, language, and structured signals to advance how machines perceive, reason about, and generate human-centered content.

At Google DeepMind, I contribute to the Gemini family of models, with a focus on multimodal understanding and expressive interaction. My work spans foundational model research, generative systems, and translating emerging capabilities into real-world applications. Across these efforts, I am driven by the goal of building AI that is technically robust, perceptive, and useful to society.

Previously, I was with Google Research. I earned my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Southern California, where I was advised by Prof. Shri Narayanan at the Signal Analysis and Interpretation Laboratory. My academic background is rooted in multimodal representation learning and affective computing. I earned my Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering from UC Santa Barbara and worked as a Junior Research Scientist at NYU Langone Medical Center, developing statistical models of functional brain networks using fMRI.

Research Areas

Multimodal & Agentic AI

Gemini 2.5 · Multimodal Reasoning · Expressive Interaction

I work on multimodal AI systems that can perceive complex environments, reason across contexts, and interact naturally in real-world settings. My contributions to the Gemini 2.5 family focus on multimodal capabilities, particularly understanding audio and human expression. I am especially interested in systems that move beyond recognizing content to understanding the context and nuance of human communication.

Generative Media & Expressivity

VideoPoet · Versatile Diffusion · Patents

I build generative models for video and audio where controllability is the core goal—timing, motion, style, and semantic fidelity. This includes VideoPoet (ICML 2024) and Versatile Diffusion (NeurIPS 2024), with an emphasis on expressivity and temporal coherence.

Computational Media Intelligence

Longitudinal Analysis · Computational Narratology · Scale

I develop methods to extract structure from large-scale media: event discovery, temporal organization, and narrative-level understanding across thousands of hours of video. This includes patented work on automated narrative analysis of movie scripts and frameworks for Computational Media Intelligence. I also discuss how this research was applied through a collaboration with the Geena Davis Institute on Gender in Media in a Google Research video feature.
